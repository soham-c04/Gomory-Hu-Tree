Theorem 4 (Page 6): Let G_o be a subgraph of an undirected graph G, and F_o,min be the minimum value of the maximum flows between all pairs of vertices in G_o. Theorem 4 can be modified to provide a result which is more useful in developing the hierarchical clustering algorithm. This result is contained in the following corollary.

Corollary 3 (Page 6): Let G_o be a subgraph of an undirected graph G. Let F'_o,min be the minimum value of the maximum flows between all pairs of vertices in G_o computed using an arbitrary subgraph, G'_o, which contains G_o, and let s and t be an arbitrary pair of vertices in G. If the value, F_st of the maximum flow between s and t, computed using G, is smaller than F'_o,min, then F_st can be equivalently computed from the condensed graph G_c with all the vertices in G_o condensed into a single vertex.

The implication of Corollary 3 is significant since the condition under which a subgraph, G_o , may be condensed can be checked using only a local subgraph which contains G_o. Using this resuly a hierarchical clustering method may be developed which uses subgraphs for condensation (local processing) and the Gomory-Hu algorithm for optimal clustering applied to the condensed graph (global processing). The theoretical foundation of such a clustering technique is presented in Theorem 5.

Theorem 5 (Page 6-7): Let G_o be a subgraph of an undirected graph G, and F'_o,min be the minimum value of the maximum flows between all pairs of vertices in G_o computed in a subgraph G'_o which contains G_o. Let G_c be the graph formed from G with all vertices in G_o condensed into a single vertex and let T*_c be an equivalent tree constructed from G_c. Then T*_c is a partially cut and flow equivalent tree of G, at threshold F'_o,min, in the sense that the maximum flow, F_st and the corresponding minimum cut can be computed from T*_c for all pairs of vertices s and t in G such that F_st < F'_o,min. Furthermore for all vertex pairs, s and t, in G with flow F_st >= F'_o,min, the maximum flow value computed in T_c is also greater than or equal to F'_o,min.

So far, we have derived sufficient conditions for subgraph condensation when we are only interested in finding minimum cuts with small cut values. In the remainder of this subsection, we will derive additional conditions for subgraph condensation with no cut value constraints attached.
Let G'_o be a subgraph of an undirected graph G and T'*_o be an equivalent tree computed from G'_o. We wish to construct an equivalent tree T* for G using as much information from T'*_o as possible. Specifically, we want to identify those minimum cuts in T'*_o which are also minimum cuts in T*. If successful, we can initialise the Gomory-Hu algorithm at a much more advanced stage, since these identified minimum cuts need not be recomputed, and thus greatly reduce the computation time.

Definition: An interior vertex of a subgraph G'_o of G is a vertex such that all of its neighbouring vertices also belong to G'_o. A non-interior vertex is called a boundary vertex.


Theorem 6 (Page 7): Let T'*_o be an equivalent tree of G'_o, a subgraph of an undirected graph G, and T*_o be a branch of T'*_o consisting exclusively of interior vertices of G'_o. Let G_c be the graph formed from G with all vertices in T*_o condensed into a single vertex v*_o. The equivalent tree T* of G can be constructed as follows: construct an equivalent tree T*_c for the condensed graph G_c, and then replace the vertex v*_o by T*_c connected at its root vertex.

Here, the root vertex of the branch T*_o refers to the only vertex in T*_o which neighbors a boundary vertex in T'*_o.
